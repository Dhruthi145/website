Who We Are

About Viraj Holdings

Viraj Holdings is a diversified business group focused on building integrated enterprises across agriculture, food, commodities, manufacturing, and international trade. We believe that sustainable growth is created by connecting industries, strengthening supply chains, and delivering value at every stage of the business ecosystem.

Founded with a vision to create businesses that are resilient, scalable, and globally competitive, Viraj Holdings brings together multiple ventures under one unified purpose — to serve markets with quality, reliability, and long-term commitment.

Our foundation lies in agriculture through Viraj Agro Inputs Pvt. Ltd., where we work to support farmers with dependable agricultural input solutions. Building on this foundation, we are expanding into commodity trading, milling, FMCG, and export–import operations, creating an ecosystem where every business complements the next.

Rather than operating as independent ventures, our businesses are designed to work together. From agricultural inputs that support production, to commodity procurement, value-added processing, consumer products, and global exports, we are developing an integrated value chain that creates efficiency, consistency, and lasting partnerships.

At the heart of Viraj Holdings is a simple philosophy: businesses should create value not only for customers, but also for farmers, suppliers, employees, business partners, and the communities they serve. We believe that trust is earned through transparency, quality is built through discipline, and growth is sustained through continuous innovation and responsible decision-making.

Viraj Holdings is more than a collection of businesses. It is a long-term vision to build an organization that connects agriculture with industry, local expertise with global markets, and today’s opportunities with tomorrow’s possibilities.

Our Vision

To build one of India’s most trusted and diversified business groups by creating integrated enterprises that connect agriculture, manufacturing, trade, and global markets. We aspire to drive sustainable growth, foster innovation, and create lasting value for our customers, partners, communities, and future generations.

Our Mission

Our mission is to build and grow businesses that deliver quality, reliability, and long-term value across every sector we operate in. We are committed to strengthening supply chains, empowering farmers and business partners, embracing innovation, and conducting every aspect of our business with integrity, transparency, and operational excellence.

Our Businesses · Agriculture Inputs

Viraj Agro Inputs Pvt. Ltd.

Empowering Agriculture. Enabling Growth.

Agriculture is the backbone of every nation, and every successful harvest begins with the right support. At Viraj Agro Inputs Pvt. Ltd., we help farmers and agribusinesses grow with confidence by providing quality agricultural inputs, modern farming solutions, technical expertise, and dependable market support.

As part of Viraj Holdings, our purpose is simple — to build a stronger agricultural ecosystem where farmers, dealers, distributors, and business partners grow together.

Who We Are

Viraj Agro Inputs Pvt. Ltd. is an integrated agricultural solutions company committed to improving productivity, profitability, and sustainability across the farming ecosystem.

We believe agriculture is not just about selling products. It is about understanding crops, solving field challenges, improving yields, and building lasting relationships. Every product we supply and every service we provide is designed to help farmers make better decisions and achieve better results.

What We Do

We provide comprehensive agricultural solutions that support every stage of the farming cycle — from preparing the land to harvesting the crop and connecting it to the market.

Seeds

Carefully selected seed varieties and hybrids that deliver strong germination, healthy crop establishment, and improved yield potential.

Crop Nutrition

Fertilizers, micronutrients, and specialty nutrition products that improve soil fertility and strengthen plant growth.

Crop Protection

Reliable solutions that help farmers safeguard crops against insects, diseases, weeds, and environmental challenges.

Organic & Sustainable Solutions

Organic fertilizers, biofertilizers, biostimulants, and biological crop solutions that improve long-term soil health.

Farm Mechanization

Custom hiring services including drone spraying, transplanting, and harvesting support that improve efficiency.

Crop Procurement & Market Linkage

Connecting agricultural produce with reliable buyers, creating stronger value for farmers beyond the harvest.

Our Approach

Every farm is different. That’s why we don’t believe in one-size-fits-all solutions. Our approach begins with understanding the farmer, the crop, and the field. Based on this understanding, we recommend suitable products, provide technical guidance, monitor crop performance, and continue supporting our customers throughout the cultivation cycle. We believe that continuous support creates better outcomes than one-time product sales.

Farmers Agricultural Dealers Distributors Retail Input Stores Farmer Producer Organizations (FPOs) Commercial Growers Agribusinesses Institutional Buyers

Complete Agricultural Solutions

Quality You Can Trust

We carefully source and deliver products that meet high standards of quality and performance.

Technical Expertise

Our experienced team offers practical recommendations based on real field conditions.

Farmer-Centric Philosophy

Our success is measured by the success of the farmers we serve.

Long-Term Partnerships

We value relationships over transactions, building trust that grows year after year.

Our Vision

We envision an agricultural future where innovation, sustainability, and collaboration work together to create stronger farms, resilient supply chains, and globally competitive agricultural businesses. Our goal is to become a trusted agricultural solutions partner that contributes to the growth of Indian agriculture while creating long-term value for every stakeholder we serve.

Together, let’s cultivate stronger farms, stronger businesses, and a stronger future.

Commodity Sourcing & Trading

Building Reliable Supply Chains. Connecting Agriculture with Markets.

Agricultural commodities are the backbone of the global food supply chain. Through Viraj Agro Inputs Pvt. Ltd., our Commodity Sourcing & Trading division connects producers, processors, manufacturers, and buyers through efficient procurement, quality assurance, and reliable supply chain management.

Our objective is simple — to create transparent and dependable trading relationships that benefit every stakeholder, from the farmer at the source to the business at the destination.

Overview

Agriculture does not end at harvest. The real value of a crop is realized when it reaches the right market, at the right quality, and at the right time. Through our commodity sourcing and trading operations, we bridge this gap by creating efficient market linkages between agricultural producers and domestic as well as international buyers.

What We Do

Commodity Procurement

Commodity Sourcing

A sourcing network that gives businesses access to reliable agricultural products for their operational needs.

Domestic Trading

International Trade Support

Quality & Supply Chain Management

Systematic quality assessment, grading, handling, and logistics coordination for consistent standards.

Milling & Agro Industries

From seed processing to cotton ginning, we focus on preserving quality, improving efficiency, and creating products that meet the expectations of farmers, industries, and domestic as well as international markets.

Our Processing Capabilities

Seed Processing

Cleaning, grading, and treating seeds to ensure high standards of purity, germination, and performance.

Cotton Ginning

Separating cotton fibres from seed while preserving fibre quality and minimizing contamination.

Cleaning & Grading

Every commodity evaluated, cleaned, and graded to standard before storage, processing, or distribution.

Packaging & Handling

Processes designed to maintain product integrity while supporting efficient transportation and storage.

Industries We Support

Agriculture Seed Companies Textile Industry Cotton Traders Commodity Traders Food Processing Companies FMCG Manufacturers Exporters Industrial Buyers
