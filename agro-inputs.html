Overview

Building resilient farms with reliable inputs and advisory support.

Viraj Agro Inputs Pvt. Ltd. supports farmers with a balanced portfolio of seeds, crop nutrition, crop protection products, mechanization solutions, and expert guidance tailored to field realities.

The business focuses on productivity, crop health, and responsible usage while strengthening the long-term performance of agriculture ecosystems.
