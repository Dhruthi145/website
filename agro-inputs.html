Agro Inputs

Empowering Agriculture. Enabling Growth.

Agriculture is the backbone of every nation, and every successful harvest begins with the right support. At Agro Inputs, we help farmers and agribusinesses grow with confidence by providing quality agricultural inputs, modern farming solutions, technical expertise, and dependable market support.

As part of Viraj Holdings, our purpose is simple—to build a stronger agricultural ecosystem where farmers, dealers, distributors, and business partners grow together.

Who We Are

Committed to improving productivity, profitability, and sustainability across the farming ecosystem.

Agro Inputs is an integrated agricultural solutions company committed to improving productivity, profitability, and sustainability across the farming ecosystem.

We believe agriculture is not just about selling products. It is about understanding crops, solving field challenges, improving yields, and building lasting relationships. Every product we supply and every service we provide is designed to help farmers make better decisions and achieve better results.

What We Do

Comprehensive Agricultural Solutions

We provide comprehensive agricultural solutions that support every stage of the farming cycle—from preparing the land to harvesting the crop and connecting it to the market.

🌱

Seeds

Quality begins with the seed. We offer carefully selected seed varieties and hybrids that deliver strong germination, healthy crop establishment, and improved yield potential.

🧪

Crop Nutrition

Healthy soil produces healthy crops. Our range of fertilizers, micronutrients, and specialty nutrition products helps improve soil fertility, strengthen plant growth, and maximize productivity.

🛡️

Crop Protection

We provide reliable crop protection solutions that help farmers safeguard their crops against insects, diseases, weeds, and environmental challenges while maintaining crop quality.

🌿

Organic & Sustainable Solutions

We support environmentally responsible farming through organic fertilizers, biofertilizers, biostimulants, and biological crop solutions that improve long-term soil health and sustainability.

🚜

Farm Mechanization

Modern farming requires modern solutions. Our custom hiring services include drone spraying, transplanting, harvesting support, and other farm equipment that improve efficiency while reducing labour costs.

📈

Crop Procurement & Market Linkage

Our relationship with farmers continues beyond the harvest. Through procurement and market linkage initiatives, we help connect agricultural produce with reliable buyers, creating better opportunities and stronger value for farmers.

Our Approach

Continuous Support Across Every Cultivation Cycle

Tailored for Every Farm

Every farm is different. That's why we don't believe in one-size-fits-all solutions.

Our approach begins with understanding the farmer, the crop, and the field. Based on this understanding, we recommend suitable products, provide technical guidance, monitor crop performance, and continue supporting our customers throughout the cultivation cycle.

Core Philosophy

We believe that continuous support creates better outcomes than one-time product sales.

1

Understand

Deeply evaluating the farmer, the crop characteristics, and field conditions.

2

Recommend & Guide

Recommending suitable products and delivering field-verified technical guidance.

3

Monitor & Support

Monitoring crop performance and offering ongoing support throughout cultivation.
